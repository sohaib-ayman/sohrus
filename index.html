Need to click behind the notch? Hold one key.

Ever had a browser tab or window button sitting right under a desktop notch? In Sohrus, hold your Temporary Hide Key and the notch instantly vanishes and becomes click-through — release it and Sohrus returns right where it was.

Alt (Default) Mouse 4 / Back Mouse 5 / Forward Mouse Middle Any Single Key

Smart by design

Configure it in Settings → Appearance → Temporary hide key, or turn it off anytime. Sohrus automatically disables ghost-hiding while you are typing inside Ask Sohrus, To-Do forms, or Scratchpad so it never interrupts your input.

Built in Pure Rust. Never crosses 30 MB of RAM.

No hidden Chromium browser, no Electron wrapper, and no WebView2 process running in the background. Sohrus renders natively with Win32, Direct2D, and DirectWrite — stopping frame draws completely when idle.
